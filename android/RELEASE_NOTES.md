## What's new in PlayReport 1.4
- Instant pages: analysed matches, team pages and day history are cached on your phone, so they open at once (even offline) and refresh in the background.
- Updates keep your history: tickets, favourites and settings are stored natively and restored after an update or re-install. New versions announce themselves in the app with their release notes.
- Download match stats as CSV: every analysed match has a "Download stats (CSV)" button (Google Sheets friendly), and the ☰ menu can download the whole day's analysis.
- Bets of the day rebuilt: strong on Over 1.5 & team goals; 1X2, Both teams to score and Over 2.5 only appear with strong supporting form. One market per match on the whole card.
- One tracked market per match in the performance record, so the markets can be compared fairly.
- Every publication is checked before it reaches the app (probabilities, prices, rules, files); a failed check keeps the previous data.
- More league history: past seasons are archived from Livescore for every covered competition (head-to-head, form and league pages fill up faster).
