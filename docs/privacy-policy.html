Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 30 October 2026

This policy explains how Mr 2 Odds, operated by Ndumiso Msani in South Africa ("we", "us"), handles information in its Android football-analysis app, this website and its optional creator publishing tools. Mr 2 Odds was previously branded PlayReport. The Android package identifier remains com.playreport.app.

At a glance: the Android app does not require an account. Your favourites, settings and private ticket records are stored on your device, not submitted to an account database operated by us. Network providers still receive ordinary request information. Contacting us or connecting a creator’s Google account involves additional information as explained below.

1. Information we handle

Records stored on your device

The app stores preferences such as theme, timezone and notification choices; favourite/tracked matches; private ticket and bet-slip records, including selections, odds, optional stakes and settlement status; and cached football analysis, scores and news. It uses Android app preferences and WebView local storage so these features can work between visits. The current app does not include a login system or a feature that uploads these private ticket records to us.

Android may back up eligible app preferences to your Google account or transfer them to another device, depending on your device settings. Our Android backup configuration includes app preferences rather than downloaded update files or the general file cache. Backup and restoration are handled by Android and your backup provider, not a Mr 2 Odds cloud-account service.

Network and device request information

The app fetches published analysis and update information from GitHub, and match scores and badge images from football-data providers such as Livescore. These services can receive your IP address, request time, user-agent information and the requested resource or match identifiers. Opening a news source, bookmaker link, WhatsApp or another external service sends you to that service under its own terms and privacy policy.

GitHub Pages hosts this website and may collect technical request information, including IP addresses, for service operation and security. This site does not add analytics scripts, advertising pixels, sign-in forms or marketing cookies. The current Android app does not integrate an advertising SDK or third-party behavioural analytics SDK.

Information you choose to send

If you email or message us, we receive your contact details and the message, screenshots or other information you provide. Please do not send passwords, access tokens, financial-account details or unnecessary sensitive information. Files you export are saved to the location you choose using Android’s system file picker; sharing them with another app is your choice.

Permissions

The current Android app uses internet and network-state access and can ask for notification permission. It does not request access to your contacts, precise location, camera or microphone. Notifications are generated using on-device checks of public football and update information; ordinary users do not need to grant Google Drive or YouTube access for these features.

2. Why information is used

We use information to provide football analysis and score updates, preserve your chosen on-device settings and records, provide requested notifications and exports, maintain and secure the service, respond to support requests, and carry out creator publishing operations you authorise.

Where data-protection law requires a legal basis, processing may be necessary to provide a service you request, rely on your consent for optional permissions and account connections, or serve legitimate interests such as service security and responding to enquiries, subject to your rights. Legal obligations may also require us to retain or disclose particular records. You can withdraw optional consent as described below.

3. Optional Google Drive and YouTube integrations

These integrations are separate creator/operator publishing tools, not a requirement for using the Android app. An authorised operator can connect their own Google account through Google OAuth to publish football-analysis videos or hand them off through a designated Drive folder.

  • Google Drive: the code requests the drive.file scope to upload app-created video files and work with files/folders made available to the app. It uses file identifiers, names and upload metadata for this handoff. It is not designed to browse unrelated private Drive content.
  • YouTube: the direct publishing tool requests the youtube scope. This is a broad permission: it supports reading channel/video information and upload status, uploading videos and metadata, updating publication settings, and deleting videos, including temporary publishing-test clips. Do not authorise this connection unless you intend to grant these capabilities.
  • Credentials and use: OAuth access/refresh tokens are used to authenticate the authorised publishing tasks. Production automation is configured through GitHub Actions secrets; credentials must not be placed in public repository files or shared in support messages. Video IDs, publication status and operational records may be retained to track delivery and prevent duplicates. Published videos, titles and descriptions are visible according to their publication settings.

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train general-purpose artificial intelligence or machine-learning models. Google account data is used only for the authorised publishing features and necessary operation, security and support. We do not permit human reading of Google user data except with your affirmative agreement for specific data, where necessary for security or legal compliance, or for internal operations where the data has been aggregated and anonymised as permitted by the Limited Use requirements.

Mr 2 Odds’ use and transfer of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. The YouTube integration is also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and Google Privacy Policy.

You can revoke access using your Google account’s third-party connections settings. Revocation stops future authorised access once any existing access expires or is invalidated; it does not automatically delete files or videos already uploaded. Remove those through Google Drive/YouTube, and contact us to request deletion of any retained integration credentials or records we control.

4. Sharing and third-party services

We do not sell your personal information or share it for targeted advertising. Information is disclosed as needed to the services used to deliver the functions you request: GitHub for hosting, releases and configured automation; football-data/image providers for requested content; Google/YouTube for authorised publishing and device backup; and your chosen email, messaging, export or publishing services. If you use a separate handoff provider such as Zapier, that provider processes the account/file information you authorise under its own policy.

Creator publishing may send the authorised video and its caption to Facebook or another selected publishing destination. This does not upload ordinary Android users’ private tickets or favourites. We may also disclose information if legally required or reasonably necessary to protect security, rights or safety.

Third-party resources: GitHub privacy statement, Google privacy policy, Meta privacy policy and WhatsApp privacy policy. Other linked services have their own policies.

5. Retention and deletion

  • On-device records: retained until you remove them, clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, subject to Android backup/restore behaviour. The in-app “Clear saved data” button clears analysis caches; it is not a complete deletion of all tickets, favourites and preferences.
  • Backups and exports: manage device backups in Android/Google settings. Files you exported and copies sent to other people or apps must be deleted separately. An uninstall may not remove those copies or existing backups.
  • Support: messages are kept while needed to handle the enquiry and reasonable follow-up, security or legal needs. You may request deletion; we will explain if a legal requirement prevents complete removal.
  • Creator connections: credentials are retained while the publishing connection remains configured. The authorised account holder can revoke Google access and request removal of credentials and identifying operational records we control. Service-provider logs and public publications may follow separate retention rules. Public football results and published analysis can remain as part of the historical record.

6. Security and international processing

Measures include HTTPS for app network requests, restricted native network hosts, Android’s app storage controls and secret-based configuration for automated publishing. No service or device can guarantee absolute security. Protect your phone and Google account and do not publish credentials.

We operate in South Africa. Hosting and other providers may process information in other countries under their own security and data-protection arrangements. Where applicable law requires safeguards for international transfers, those requirements apply.

7. Your choices and privacy rights

You can disable notifications, adjust background activity, remove local records, clear Android app storage, manage backups, revoke creator-account access or stop using the service. There is no ordinary app account that you need to close.

Depending on applicable law, including South Africa’s POPIA and, where applicable, the GDPR, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or to object to certain processing, withdraw consent and complain to your relevant data-protection authority. Contact us below. We may need proportionate verification that a request concerns your information; do not send identity documents unless a suitable process is agreed. We cannot remotely retrieve records that exist only on your phone.

8. Adults only

Mr 2 Odds is intended for people aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly solicit personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us so we can investigate and remove information we control where appropriate.

9. Changes to this policy

We will publish updates at this URL and change the “last updated” date. Material changes will be explained here or through an appropriate service notice. If future features change what data we handle, this policy must be updated to describe them.
